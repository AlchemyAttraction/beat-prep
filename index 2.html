A beat calculator for memoir

Beat-Prep

Beat sheets are built for screenplays and novels, where a villain closes in. In a memoir the pressure comes from inside: the person you were, the person telling it, and the distance between them.

Enter your word count and you'll see where each turn of a true story should land, down to the page. Add where your draft is now to find out which beat you're writing.

Everything you type stays in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Most memoirs land between 70,000 and 90,000 words. The percentages are a map, not a law: a beat landing a few pages early or late is fine.
